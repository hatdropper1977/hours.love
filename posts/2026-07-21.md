Lawrence Wine Estates, led by owner Gaylon Lawrence of Napa, has finalized the purchase of Chateau Montelena in Calistoga. This acquisition matters because it places one of Napa Valley's most historically significant estates under new ownership. The deal ensures the continuity of a brand that gained global fame during the 1976 Judgment of Paris. It also reflects the ongoing consolidation of iconic California vineyards into large private portfolios.

Gaylon Lawrence and Master Sommelier Carlton McCoy Jr. lead the acquisition team. They purchased the property from the Barrett family. Bo Barrett will remain involved as a consultant and advisor. The transaction includes the historic castle winery and the 120-acre estate. It also includes the brand and existing wine inventory.

The estate is located in the northernmost part of the Napa Valley. It is famous for its Chardonnay and Cabernet Sauvignon. The vineyard sits on a unique mix of volcanic and alluvial soils. These conditions allow for balanced ripening and distinct acidity. The winery was built in 1888 and is carved into a hillside.

This sale highlights a trend of private investment in premium Napa properties. The [San Francisco Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com) noted that the Barrett family owned the winery for over 50 years [^1]. The new owners plan to maintain the existing winemaking team. This stability is vital for preserving the estate's stylistic identity.

Locally, the acquisition impacts the Calistoga sub-appellation. Lawrence Wine Estates already owns several nearby properties. These include Heitz Cellar, Burgess Cellars, and Stony Hill Vineyard. Combining these assets allows for shared resources in production and logistics. It also strengthens the group's position in the high-end direct-to-consumer market.

[WineBusiness](https://www.winebusiness.com) reported that the transition will focus on hospitality upgrades [^2]. The new owners intend to enhance the visitor experience at the Calistoga site. They also aim to expand the brand's international distribution. This mirrors their strategy with other heritage brands in their portfolio.

The [Napa Valley Register](https://napavalleyregister.com) mentioned that the Barrett family sought a partner with long-term vision [^3]. The deal preserves the agricultural integrity of the estate. It prevents the potential fragmentation of the historic vineyard land. The purchase price was not officially disclosed to the public. [Wine Spectator](https://www.winespectator.com) indicated the sale includes all vineyard assets and the brand name [^4].

Other recent developments include the expansion of the Lawrence hospitality team. They have hired several luxury hospitality veterans to manage their tasting rooms. This focus on premium service reflects the increasing competition in Napa Valley tourism. The group continues to seek out properties with distinct geological profiles.
