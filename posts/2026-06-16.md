Sean McGaughey and Melissa Yanc have established Molti Amici as a central fixture of the Healdsburg restaurant scene. This recognition matters because the establishment bridges the gap between casual dining and Michelin-level technique in Sonoma County. The restaurant operates at 330 Healdsburg Avenue in the former Campo Fina site. It serves as a model for the next generation of hospitality in the North Coast.

McGaughey previously served as executive sous chef at the three-starred SingleThread. Yanc is the founder of the local bakery Quail & Condor. Their partnership combines fine-dining standards with a relaxed atmosphere. The [San Francisco Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com) reported that the Michelin Guide recently added the restaurant to its California selection [^1]. This recognition highlights the quality of the wood-fired pizzas and handmade pastas. The kitchen uses ingredients from local farms and purveyors.

The menu includes rigatoni with pork ragu and crudo with seasonal citrus. These dishes reflect the seasons of the Russian River Valley. The kitchen staff includes many alumni from Michelin-starred kitchens in the Bay Area. This concentration of talent improves the regional labor pool. It also encourages professional development for junior cooks. Local residents benefit from a range of dining options at different price points.

The interior design preserves the historic character of the building. The dining room features an open kitchen and a large wood-burning oven. A popular outdoor patio includes a bocce ball court. This feature maintains the communal spirit of the previous occupant. [Sonoma Magazine](https://www.sonomamag.com) noted that the restaurant has become a social hub for winemakers [^2]. It provides a meeting place for industry professionals and tourists alike.

Healdsburg has seen a surge in luxury hospitality over the last five years. High-end hotels and tasting rooms have changed the local economy. Molti Amici provides a business model that attracts repeat local customers. This balance is crucial for the long-term health of the downtown area. The restaurant creates year-round employment for culinary staff. It also supports the regional agricultural network through consistent sourcing.

The beverage program focuses on Sonoma County and European labels. Wine Director Chloe Sosebee curates a list that emphasizes small-scale producers. [WineBusiness](https://www.winebusiness.com) indicated that the restaurant’s wine program reflects the diverse terroir of the Russian River Valley [^3]. It offers guests a chance to discover labels that are not widely distributed. The bar also serves cocktails using local spirits and seasonal fruit.

Other recent developments in Healdsburg include the opening of Goodnight’s Prime Steak. Several new tasting rooms have also opened on the plaza. These businesses collectively increase the town’s appeal as a food destination. The competition drives innovation among local chefs. It also pressures the city to improve parking and public infrastructure.
